Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how Foolhard Mail Agent, a private, owner-operated email automation tool, processes data when its owner authorizes Gmail API access.

1. Information accessed

Depending on permissions granted and features enabled, Foolhard Mail Agent may access message content, subjects, sender and recipient addresses, timestamps, message IDs, labels, and related metadata. OAuth credentials are used to access the authorized account.

2. How information is used

Gmail data is used to identify task instructions, run authorized automations, respond to messages, and send task-related notifications. Message labels or status may be updated when authorized. Gmail data is not sold or used for advertising or advertising profiles.

3. Data sharing and AI processing

The application accesses Google APIs to carry out authorized mail operations. If the owner enables AI-assisted processing, relevant task content may be processed by the AI service provider configured by the owner to complete the requested task. Gmail data is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials, processing state, and operational logs may be stored in the owner's private computing environment to support the service, troubleshoot failures, and maintain continuity. Logs may contain task-related message information. The owner manages retention and can delete these local records when no longer needed.

5. Security

Access to stored credentials and processing records is intended to be restricted to the authorized owner and their configured execution environment. No method of data transmission or storage is guaranteed perfectly secure.

6. Revocation and deletion

The account owner can stop the tool, remove locally stored credentials and records, and revoke Gmail access at Google Account connections. For questions or deletion requests, contact the owner below.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.
