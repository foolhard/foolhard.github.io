Gmail Assistant

Private email automation for the authorized account owner.

Gmail Assistant is an owner-operated tool that uses the Gmail API to receive task instructions by email, process them through configured workflows, and send replies or task notifications.

What it does

  • Reads authorized Gmail messages and relevant metadata to find task instructions.
  • Processes owner-configured tasks, including AI-assisted workflows when enabled.
  • Sends responses and notifications from the authorized Gmail account.
  • May update message labels or status, only with permissions explicitly granted by the account owner.

Authorization and control

Gmail Assistant is designed for personal use, not as a public email service. Access requires the account owner's consent through Google OAuth. The owner may revoke access through Google Account settings at any time.
